What this screen does
Download your Order History Items from Vetcove (Orders → Export → Items). Upload it here and MedSync logs each completed line item to your COGS spend. The preview below shows budget vs. spend week-by-week for the calendar year, using the same revenue × COGs% formula as the Procurement screen. Cancelled, backordered, and $0 items are excluded automatically.
