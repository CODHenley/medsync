What this screen does
Download your Order History from Vetcove (not return credits — use the Vetcove Import screen for those). Upload it here and MedSync will log each completed purchase to your Cost of Goods spend, which flows into the Analytics COGs chart and Purchase History panel. Cancelled, backordered, and $0 orders are excluded automatically. Returns are logged as negative spend to offset.
